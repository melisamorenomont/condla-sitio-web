`rangoAnos` cell. Also fixed: Plot/Inputs.table were showing years with
thousands separators (e.g. "2,004") -- added `tickFormat: "d"` and a
table `format` override. Cost if wrong: none, these are output-formatting
corrections with no semantic effect on the data.
